GisTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

GisTV ("GisTV", "we", "us", or "our") is an IPTV player application for Android TV. This Privacy Policy explains what information the GisTV app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have. It describes the app as it is actually built; where a feature is not active in the current release, we say so.

In short: GisTV stores your playlists, channels, favorites, EPG data, viewing history and provider credentials in a local database on your device. Accounts and sign-in are local to your device in this version. We do not operate a cloud account server for GisTV in this release. The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, Google AdMob (with Google's consent tool), and an Adsterra ad network for advertising, and fetches programme metadata from TMDB. You can delete your account and its local data from inside the app.

1. Information you provide

  • Account information. If you create an optional local Gis account, you provide a username and a password. The password itself is never stored; see section 4. A "remember me" preference may also be stored.
  • Playlists and provider details. If you add an M3U playlist or an Xtream Codes provider, you provide the playlist URL or server address, port, and (for Xtream) a username and password. These are used to connect to the IPTV provider you chose.
  • Optional credentials scanning. If you use the credential scanner, you may grant camera access so the app can read provider credentials from an image. Text recognition runs on your device; images are not uploaded to us.

2. Information stored on your device

GisTV keeps its data in its private app storage. This includes:

  • Room (SQLite) databases holding playlists, channels, favourite groups, EPG data, viewing history, and the provider credentials attached to your playlist rows;
  • app preferences (including the sign-in/ownership state and the last username used for convenience);
  • an image cache used to speed up artwork loading.

Data is separated by slot: the guest slot (used before you sign in or when you choose "Continue as Guest") and one isolated database per Gis account. Your account data is not mixed with guest data.

3. Device and technical information

To operate and diagnose the app, GisTV may process device and app information such as a random, app-generated device identifier (not a hardware serial), the device model name, the app version, the operating-system/API level, and general network state. This information is used to run the app, keep a stable identity for the local account, and show diagnostics.

4. Accounts and sign-in (local to your device)

In this version, GisTV accounts are local to your device. When you create or sign in to an account:

  • The account identifier is derived deterministically from your username (a UUIDv5 value). The same username maps to the same identifier across Gis apps.
  • Your password is verified on the device using PBKDF2-SHA256 with 120,000 iterations. Only a random salt, the derived verifier and the iteration count are kept; the plaintext password is never written to storage.
  • This sign-in material is stored using Android Keystore-backed encrypted preferences.

Signed-in or not, your playlists, favourites, viewing history and provider credentials remain in the local database described above.

5. Do we send your data to our servers?

GisTV does not contact a cloud account server in this release. The app's cloud-sync code is present in the source but is not connected in this build, so no account data, playlist, favourite or credential is transmitted to a server operated by us. Account creation, sign-in and account deletion are performed entirely on your device.

Local network sync. When you explicitly link GisTV with a compatible app (such as the Windows app) on the same Wi-Fi network, playlists and settings are transferred directly between the two devices over your local network. This transfer does not go through a server operated by us.

Your IPTV provider. When you use a playlist or Xtream provider, your device connects directly to that provider's servers using the credentials you entered. That provider's handling of your data is governed by its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

6. Third-party services

The app includes the following third-party components, which may process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Analytics — anonymous app usage and app-open events, an app-instance identifier, and device/app diagnostics, to help us understand usage and fix issues.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error diagnostics to help us fix bugs. The app also writes a local crash log on the device.
  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — advertising and consent management. See section 7.
  • Adsterra — an additional ad network that may be used to serve ads in the app.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) — programme and artwork metadata is requested from TMDB's public API to enrich channel information.
  • GitHub — the app checks GitHub for software updates.
  • iptv-org / jsDelivr — public, freely available playlist and channel-list sources that you may choose to browse.

We do not send your playlists, favourites, viewing history, or provider credentials to these services.

7. Advertising and consent

GisTV may show advertising to support development. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), Google's User Messaging Platform is used to request and manage your consent before the ads SDK serves personalized ads. The ad networks may process an advertising identifier and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. You can change your consent choices through the consent form when it is presented, and you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's system settings.

8. Analytics and crash reporting

Firebase Analytics is initialized when the app starts and Crashlytics collects crash diagnostics. These help us keep GisTV stable. We do not use them to build advertising profiles of your IPTV content, and we do not sell your personal information.

9. Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network / Wi-Fi state — to load playlists and streams, fetch metadata, and support local network linking.
  • Camera (optional) — only for the credential-scanning feature; you can decline it.
  • Notifications and foreground media playback — to keep playback running and show playback controls.
  • Read media — to let you browse your own photo/video media where you choose to.
  • Install updates — used only to apply an app update on builds distributed outside an app store; store builds open the releases page instead.

10. Data retention

Local data (playlists, favourites, viewing history, EPG, provider credentials and account material) is retained on your device until you delete your account in the app, clear the app's data in system settings, or uninstall the app. There is no server-side copy for us to retain in this release.

11. Account and data deletion

You can delete your Gis account and its local data from inside the app. Because GisTV accounts are local to your device in this version, deletion is performed on your device; no server-side deletion occurs. 12. Your choices

  • Use "Continue as Guest" to use the app without creating an account.
  • Delete your account and its local data from the in-app deletion flow (section 11).
  • Clear all app data or uninstall the app to remove everything it stores locally.
  • Manage ad consent via the consent form and your device's advertising settings.

13. Children's privacy

GisTV is a general-audience IPTV player and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will review and, where appropriate, delete it.

14. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by GisTV, including Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage for sign-in material. However, no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published at this location with a revised "Last updated" date.
