GisTV Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Gis account and associated data in the GisTV Android TV app, what is deleted, and what is kept.

Important: In the current version of GisTV, accounts and sign-in are local to your device. GisTV does not operate a cloud account server in this release, so deleting your account removes your account and its data from your device. There is no separate server-side copy of your account for us to delete.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open GisTV and sign in to the account you want to delete (deletion only appears when you are signed in).
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. In the account section, choose Delete Account.
  4. Confirm in the dialog. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

When deletion succeeds, the app returns to Guest mode.

What is deleted

  • The account's local database (playlists, channels, favourites, EPG data, viewing history, and the provider credentials stored on your playlist rows), together with its write-ahead/shared-memory/journal sidecar files.
  • Account-scoped provider (xtream_*) credentials.
  • The encrypted sign-in vault for the account: username, password salt and verifier, iteration count, and the "remember me" preference.
  • The account identifier held in the device's app state.
  • Any legacy database copies that were claimed into the account.

What is kept

  • Device-owned identity that is not tied to your account: the random device identifier and device name.
  • Guest data and any other local account on the device (deletion only removes the account you are signed in to).
  • The app installation itself, and app preferences that are not account-scoped.

If deletion does not complete

Deletion is designed to be truthful: if any of the account's database files cannot be removed, the app does not report success and does not clear your credentials. Nothing is removed, you stay signed in, and you can try again.

If you cannot access the app

Because GisTV accounts are local to the device, we cannot remotely delete an account that exists only on your device. If you cannot open the app, you can still remove all locally stored data using your device:

  • Open Android Settings → Apps → GisTV → Storage and choose Clear data; or
  • uninstall GisTV. Uninstalling removes the app's local databases, preferences and cache.

If you believe account information may persist and need help, contact us using the details below and describe your situation.

What is not performed

No server-side account deletion is performed, because GisTV does not contact a cloud account server in this release. The app's cloud-sync code is present in the source but is not connected in this build.

Data retention

After deletion, the account's data described above is removed from your device. Device identifiers and guest/other-account data remain until you clear app data or uninstall.
