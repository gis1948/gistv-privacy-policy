GisTV Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: September 20, 2026

GisTV users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to gis1948@gmail.com with the subject:

GisTV Account Deletion Request

Include the email address or username associated with your GisTV account so that we can identify the account and process the request.

Account and Associated Data

After the request is verified, GisTV will delete or remove the personal account information associated with the account, subject to information that may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate obligations.

Contact

For account deletion requests, contact: gis1948@gmail.com
